An analysis of your PDF parsing script (`parse_pdf.py`) reveals **two major engineering gaps** that explain why financial tables and multi-year data rows are getting fractured and causing retrieval failures (`q006`, `q007`, `q011`).

---

### **Identified Gaps & Causes in `parse_pdf.py**`

#### **1. Token Limit Too Low (`MAX_TOKENS = 512`)**

* **The Issue:** Your script sets `MAX_TOKENS = 512`. Financial tables in Form 10-K filings (such as the Consolidated Statements of Income or Segment Tables) contain dozens of rows and columns.
* **The Impact:** When `HybridChunker` processes a dense financial table, a limit of 512 tokens forces it to split large tables across multiple consecutive chunks. This splits related rows and column values (e.g., separating Net Income or Revenues for 2026, 2025, and 2024 into separate chunks), causing our multi-year and comparative queries to fail.

#### **2. Aggressive Chunk Merging & Row Orphanization (`merge_peers=True`)**

* **The Issue:** `HybridChunker(..., merge_peers=True)` attempts to merge adjacent text elements. However, in financial documents parsed from complex PDFs, table rows can lose their spatial alignment or headers when merged without table-aware cell preservation.
* **The Impact:** Labels (e.g., `Income tax expense`) get orphaned from their numerical values (e.g., `792`) or get split across chunks, resulting in uninterpretable plain-text chunks like `Income tax expense, YEAR ENDED MAY 31,.2026 = `.

---

### **Recommended Fixes in `parse_pdf.py**`

1. **Increase `MAX_TOKENS` for Financial Documents:**
* Increase token capacity for financial chunks to **1024 or 2048 tokens** so that entire financial statements and tables fit cleanly inside single chunks without being sliced in half.


2. **Optimize Chunker Parameters:**
* Adjust or disable aggressive peer merging for tables (`merge_peers=False` for table components) to preserve intact rows.


3. **Use Table-to-Markdown Serialization:**
* Ensure tables are serialized as coherent Markdown tables rather than flat key-value text strings so that embedding models capture the full row-column relationship.
